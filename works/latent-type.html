06 — Corpus —

Each real typeface is one point on this map, placed by an encoder that has seen only its 36 bitmaps. A decoder, told which letter to draw, turns any point into a 32 × 32 probability field. Walk between the points and it draws typefaces that were never designed. The weights are steps along the one direction the model found to mean "more ink"; the map is rotated so that direction points right.
