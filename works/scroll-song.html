P14 · SCROLL SONG · THE PARAPEITO READER · AN EXPERIMENT IN THREE INTERVIEWS

Reading is playing.

This long page is one bossa nova song. The red line is the playhead: whatever you are reading is the bar you hear. Read fast and the band speeds up; stop, and the chord waits for you; stay, and the song grows.

  1. 1 Scroll to playScroll or wheel as you normally read. The text crossing the red line chooses the chord. Your reading speed sets the tempo (±30%).
  2. 2 Stop to lingerStay on a paragraph: after 4 s a flute joins, after 10 s richer chords and a hidden margin note open. Click a paragraph to loop it. Hold the mouse down for a fermata.
  3. 3 CompareTurn the wordmark knob for volume. Press X to switch to an ordinary page with background music, then tell the card whether reading changed.

Speakers at a low level or headphones. SPACE stops every sound, M mutes, I shows what each element does. Nothing is recorded except your own WORKS / DOESN'T notes, kept in this browser.

        Spec — Scroll Song

        TYPEFACES

        Gloock — a high-contrast display serif with a printed, slightly inky texture. It carries the artists' voices and the wordmark ring: a reading face that still looks like ink on paper when the fault shifts it sideways.

        Inconsolata (variable width 50–200, weight 200–900) — the interviewer and the machine: questions, chord symbols, bar numbers, measures. Condensed widths (80–92%) for readouts so numbers stay aligned. Two faces = two voices: who speaks, and what the page is doing about it.

        MAPPING RULES

        Song position = paragraph under the red line (42% of viewport height). Each paragraph owns its chords; its height is divided evenly into bars.

        Reading pace = words crossing the line over the last 8 s. Tempo = base × (0.7 + 0.6 × min(wpm/500, 1)) → ±30%. No scrolling for 8 s → SUSTAIN: rhythm section drops out, the chord rings and re-voices slowly.

        Dwell (per paragraph, experiment ON): ≥ 4 s → flute melody for that paragraph; ≥ 10 s → colour tones (added 9th / 13th) + the artist's margin note unfolds.

        Volume knob: Richter-style log magnitude M 0–6. Gain = 10^((M−6)/4): every whole step is +5 dB. Every whole step shifts alternating paragraphs one 7 px notch — the page faults as it gets louder.

        Cursor speed → strum: guitar attack, string spread (28 → 8 ms) and brightness. Text selection → flute melody: one note per word, pitch by word length on the current chord's scale.

        Chord changes are quantised to the next beat; the band never jumps mid-beat.

        MOTION

        Trace pulse 360 ms · fault jolt 160 ms (3 steps) · margin note unfold 180 ms · coach marks 3.4 s each · no fades.

        COLOUR

        Paper #FBF7EE · ink #1D1915 · terracotta #C4532D only for what is live: the playhead, the current bar, dwell, the lit part of the wordmark.
