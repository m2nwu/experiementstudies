    Room Type — specification

    Typefaces

    Newsreader (variable: opsz 6–72, wght 200–800). A text serif with a real optical-size axis: at opsz 6 it is a sturdy, low-contrast caption cut; at 72 it is a hairline display cut. That span is the acoustic span from dead to ringing — the serifs and hairlines behave like sustain.

    Spline Sans Mono. The measuring voice: every number, label and gloss. Fixed widths read like a meter; it never responds to the room, so it stays the ruler against which the serif moves.

    Mapping (RT60 → type)

    Leading: 0.5 s = 100%, 2 s = 300% (linear, 133 points per second), below 0.5 s down to 80% at 0 s, capped at 300%. Headline leading = 0.92 + 0.3 × (lineup leading − 1).

    Optical size: 6 + 66 × k, weight: 760 − 520 × k, with k = ln(RT60 / 0.2) / ln(30) — log, because hearing is log.

    Letter-spacing: −0.025 em + 0.045 em × k. Word space: normal − 0.04 em + 0.05 em per second.

    Echo tail: steps = round(2 × RT60), max 12. The tail falls into the white space the room has opened: copy i of every word is shifted right by i/steps × 0.19 em per second and down by i/steps × 45% of the extra leading, drawn as a hairline outline at opacity a × (1 − i / (steps + 1)), a = 0.8 × min(1, 4/steps)^0.75 so twelve copies weigh about as much as four. Discrete copies, never a blur or a fade. In long rooms the last copies reach the next word and the line below — that is where reading starts to smear, as C50 predicts.

    UI outline: superellipse corner, exponent n = 14 − 11.4 × k, radius 4 + 38 × k px. Poster gets round(steps/2) inner echo outlines, 6 px apart.

    Clarity C50 = 10·log10(e^(0.69 / RT60) − 1) dB (Sabine decay). Below 0 dB the late sound outweighs the direct sound: on screen this is where tails start covering the next word.

    Sound

    Choir: four formant voices (S A T B), each two detuned saws through three band-pass formants for the vowels a o u e i. The choir sings the vowels of the headline in order; the sung word is underlined.

    Harmonic rhythm follows the room: RT60 under 0.9 s → pulsed syllables at 112 bpm (dry rooms keep notes apart); otherwise one chord every 1.2 + 1.1 × RT60 s (up to 8 s) — the reason chant is slow in stone.

    Reverb: one convolution kernel generated in code for the exact position: noise × e^(−6.91 t / RT60), with early reflections, high-frequency damping and tunnel flutter blended by room weights; reflectors add spikes at 8–80 ms. Kernels cross over in 120 ms.

    Room blend: weights 1 / (d + 3)³ by distance to each room's walls; RT60 is the weighted geometric mean.

    Motion

    Trace pulses 360 ms. Shout copies appear every RT60 / steps seconds and vanish together at RT60. Keys 1–5 walk the listener in 520 ms. No fades anywhere: every change is a step.

    World

    Hollow Chapter is a fictional choral label that tours one programme through five rooms: Cupboard No. 3 (0.2 s), The Low Cellar (0.6 s), Hall K (1.4 s), St. Ebba's Nave (3.2 s), Wyre Line Tunnel (6.0 s). All names are invented.
