Each letter is a population of sixteen stroke genomes: nodes joined by tapered Bézier strokes. Every generation the reader (a small neural net trained in this page) scores how surely it reads the target letter; a second measure scores how far the raster sits from every training face of that letter. Fitness is reading × strangeness. Your picks get a breeding bonus, never die while picked, and pass a fading preference to their offspring. The weight axis is time: heavier weights overprint more ancestors of the same individual.
